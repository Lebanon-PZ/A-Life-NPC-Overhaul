# ===== PERFORMANCE PROFILE ==========================================================================
# How much the NPC is told every time it talks. Small models get confused by too much, so they're told less.
# By default it's picked from the model (the "profile" column above). You don't have to touch this.
#
#   lite    Who they are, their mood toward you, what's happening right now, what they remember.
#           Their group's politics are left out. Shortest prompt, fastest replies. Best for 3B models.
#   normal  Everything in lite, plus their group: how it's run, what they believe, their goals, allies
#           and enemies. Best for 4B models.
#   full    Everything: all ties, what changed since your last visit, longer memory and context.
#           Best for 7B and up.
#
# Always included, any profile: name answers, tribute demands, war with you, secret pacts and join
# offers. The big ones (war, unpaid tribute, people wanting to join or leaving your base) also show
# on screen, so the AI can never forget them.
#
# To force a profile, delete the "# " in front of ONE of these lines (put it back for automatic):
# profile=lite
# profile=normal
# profile=full
