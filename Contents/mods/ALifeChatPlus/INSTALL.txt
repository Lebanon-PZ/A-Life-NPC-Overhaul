A-LIFE NPC OVERHAUL (beta)
Project Zomboid Build 42.21
==========================

Talk to Project A-Life NPCs, give them orders, run a camp. Their groups trade,
raid, starve and settle real houses. Nothing spawns. The AI is optional:
everything works without it.


NEEDS
-----
- Project A-Life NPCs (Steam Workshop)
- Recommended: A-Life Jeem Extension (Steam Workshop)
- For the AI only: Windows, and a graphics card with 6-8 GB of video memory,
  or a cloud AI account with your own key


1. INSTALL
----------
1. Subscribe on the Steam Workshop. Steam puts it in place for you.
2. In the mod list: Project A-Life first, then the Jeem Extension, then
   A-Life NPC Overhaul. Safe to add to a save you already have.
3. Load your game.


2. FIRST STEPS IN GAME
----------------------
- Right-click > A-Life: the quick wheel for whoever (or wherever) you point at.
  The A-Life window (all the tabs) is on the wheel too.
- Talk: walk up to someone and use the chat key, or open the talk window.
- Keys: Options > Mods > A-Life NPC Overhaul. Nothing is bound at first.
  Bind "A-Life window" and "Talk to the nearest person" at least.
- Stuck? The Help tab in the A-Life window.
- Settings: A-Life window > Settings. A switch you save there stays for this
  world. "Back to sandbox values" undoes them. Sandbox pages: NPC Overhaul 1-6.


3. THE AI (optional)
--------------------
With it, people understand anything you say and answer in their own words.
Without it, plain orders work (Help > Commands lists them).

Free and local:
1. In game: A-Life window > AI tab > "Open the AI folder".
2. Double-click Setup.cmd. It installs Ollama and a model, and a small helper
   that starts the AI with the game and stops it after.
3. Back in game, the AI tab shows if it's working and lets you test it.

Cloud (your own key): same folder, double-click "Set up cloud AI.cmd",
or fill in the form on the AI tab.

Read "READ ME FIRST.txt" in that folder for what each file does.


4. SOMETHING WRONG?
-------------------
Send these on Discord or in the Workshop comments:
- console.txt  (C:\Users\<you>\Zomboid)
- Zomboid\Lua\ALifeChatPlus_health.txt
AI trouble: AI tab > Doctor check, then send Zomboid\Lua\alifeai_doctor.txt.

A feature that hits an error switches itself off for the session and tells you.
See CHANGELOG.txt for what's new.
