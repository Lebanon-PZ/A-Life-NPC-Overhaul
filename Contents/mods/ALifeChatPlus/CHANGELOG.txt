v0.9.5 (Beta, test build) - LEGACY, PART 5: last secrets (romance, walk away, seasonal nights, birthdays, letters)

- Romance, deeper: a month, a hundred days, a year with your partner: they mark it. "Will you marry me?" after long
  enough together, if they love you enough: a wedding, a feast from the real stores, in the Chronicle. Apart for
  days and back again: they run to you. Lose them and the camp knows it. Die, and a partner living at your base is
  the one who takes over.
- Walk away, the last quest: once, very rarely (a year in, at peace, a big camp that loves you), a chance to leave it
  all. The camp gives you what it can spare from its real storage and goes on without you; you're remembered as a
  Legend who walked away.
- Seasonal nights at camp: ghost stories on Halloween, a Thanksgiving feast (the real fourth Thursday), a New Year's
  Eve toast. Feasts only with food in the stores.
- Ages and birthdays: everyone has an age and a birthday; your people mention theirs, a year older.
- Letters on the dead: now and then one of a group's dead carries a letter saying where their group keeps its stash.
- Only the leader gives orders. Your next character after a death can't order the old camp's people (camp orders,
  jobs, follow / wait / guard, sending them places) until the heir hands the camp back. Joined an NPC group? Their
  people take orders from their leader, not you, until you lead them. Talking is always fine.
- No elections in your own camp: your people follow you because they love you, need you, or fear you. Votes on laws
  stay.
- The region boss lays down the law: the group that won the last war sends a messenger with its rules. A kingdom:
  no group raids another, nobody keeps people in chains. A warlord: nobody raids but them. A group that raids
  another gets hit by the boss a couple of days later, for real. You kill someone from a group that wasn't after
  you, or hold captives under a kingdom: a warning first, then the boss's people come for you. The law ends when
  the boss falls or loses its hold.

v0.9.4 (Beta, test build) - LEGACY, PART 4: the order hardens (coalitions, the last war, democracy, the fortress)

- Coalitions: when one group towers over the rest, the weaker ones near each other band together, if together they'd
  match it. A coalition breaks when its enemy is gone, a member falls, or two members fight. Its members stand with
  each other when one is attacked.
- The last war (from day 90): the two strongest sides close in power go to war. Back one (your strength fights with
  them), broker a peace (by how both see you), or stay out. A battle a week; the loser is taken in; the winners pick
  one leader for the region.
- The order hardens after the war: a kingdom, a warlord's land, a coalition, or free towns, from who holds the
  strength. Kingdoms and warlords make every weaker group near them pay; free towns end tribute. If the rulers lose
  their hold, the order breaks and war is possible again.
- Democracy: put laws to a vote ("let's vote on short rations", "vote on opening the door", "vote on no killing
  prisoners"). Each of your people votes from real things: hunger, food left, beds, who they are, what they think of
  you. No elections in your camp: they follow you because they love you, need you, or fear you.
- The fortress: when the airport stands empty, the strongest group near it wants it. Make a base there first and
  it's yours (and they hate you for it); otherwise they move in and dig in.
- 10 new settings.

FIXES
- Fix: with Project Viewpoint (first or third person) NPCs often answered "I don't understand you" to perfectly
  normal lines. The cause: the 3D view fills the graphics card (as little as ~200 MB of 8 GB left), so a local AI
  model runs much slower (one reply took 25 s instead of the usual 2 s). After 9 s A-Life answered with its own
  line, and when A-Life didn't understand the line either, that line was "I don't understand you". Now, when the

  line still follows if it comes within 30 s. Lines A-Life does understand still get its answer as before.
- Fix: a chat command typed in front of a line ("/say hi", "/s hi") was sent to the NPC as part of what you said
  ("say hi"). It's taken off now; "/shout", "/yell" and "/y" count as shouting.
- Tip for Viewpoint players with a local AI: use a smaller model (line 1 of bridge\model.txt) or cloud AI, or turn
  down Viewpoint's heaviest settings (shadows, far view) so the model fits on the card.
- Fix: small models sometimes put their reasoning or the prompt's rules into what an NPC says ('...through."} 12 words
  max. This is a single response.'). Thinking blocks, leftover JSON and sentences about the reply itself are taken
  out before the line is shown.

v0.3.2 (Beta)

  - chop trees near the base (the tree is really gone) for logs, with an axe
  - saw logs into planks at their stockpile, with a saw
  - in the evening, take doors off other houses for hinges and a doorknob, with a screwdriver
  - board their ground-floor windows (planks + nails), raise a log palisade around the house (logs), and hang a
    gate in it (planks, hinges, doorknob)
- Everything comes out of and goes into the base's stockpile container. You can see it, and you can steal it.
- No tool, no job. Missing tools or nails go on their want list, and they'll mention it when you talk to them.
- Work you don't see happens day by day and shows up when you come back: stumps, missing doors, walls up,
  materials in the stockpile. Never within 25 tiles of you, never in your base or a safehouse.
- Pre-seeded bases no longer get free boards on their windows. They board them with planks they made.
- Wood doesn't run out at the doorstep: they start within 30 tiles, and when that's bare they go on supply
  runs out to 100, 200, then 300 tiles. Far wood is carried home a couple of logs at a time, so far wood
  means slow walls.
- Everything they gather is carried home in their own hands first, then put in the stockpile.
- They also strip wooden tables, chairs, shelves and fences in empty places for planks and nails (with a
  hammer or a saw). Anything with a container in it is left alone.
- Work you don't see only uses trees, doors and furniture a scan has actually found standing. A base you've
  never been near doesn't build until you've passed by once. If something they counted is gone when you come
  back (you took it), it comes back off their ledger. No logs from nowhere.
- When there's nothing left to cut even 300 tiles out, they say they need wood.

v0.3.1 (Beta)
- New: the Airport faction (sandbox option, off by default). On the start day you choose, 10 soldiers are founded
  at the Louisville airport with one leader. They stay home and only make short trips into Louisville.
- They live on what they scavenge from Louisville and nothing else. Louisville runs dry over time; at zero food
  they starve, and every death is permanent. Wipe them out and they're gone for that save.
- Their supplies set their mood: well stocked = not interested in trade; low = hard on strangers, patient with
  people who've dealt fairly with them. (Trades and the helicopter come in 0.3.2.)
- They talk short and direct, and never say where they're based or who they answer to.
- Fix: "AI helper isn't running" even after Setup. An old copy of the helper (from an earlier version) could keep
  running and block the new one, so Setup did nothing. The helper now stops old copies when it starts. New
  "Restart AI helper.cmd" in the bridge folder, and a log at Zomboid\Lua\alifeai_helper_log.txt.
- Sandbox page "A-Life Chat Plus": on/off, start day, and airport X/Y (0 = found from the map's room names).

v0.2.6 (Beta, first Workshop release)
First public test release. See the Workshop description or INSTALL.txt for the full feature list.
New in this build:
- Chat fix: in some single-player games vanilla chat throws "hasCapability of non-table: null" when you press Enter, so the box stayed open and nothing showed over your head. The mod now closes the box and shows what you said over your head when that happens.
- "Nobody is close enough to hear you" note on screen when you type or speak and no NPC is in earshot (before, it did nothing, which looked like the chat was broken).
- Fix: the dead-body check and the pickup car scan errored on 42.21 (vehicle/object lists changed). They now read the lists the 42.21 way.
- Talk key is now changeable: edit bridge\ptt_key.txt (CapsLock, Mouse4, Mouse5, RightCtrl, F1-F12, any letter). Holding it under 0.75 seconds is ignored, so taps and typing do nothing (the old tap = "Okay." is gone).
- Easy setup: Setup.cmd installs Ollama, the AI model, and a hidden helper that starts the AI and voice with the game and stops them when it closes.
- The game tells you what failed (top-left corner + over your head), with the reason.
- Driving: "get in", "get out", "take us to <town, place>". Only where the map is loaded.
- Pickups over the walkie using a real car seen near the NPC (no spawned cars).
- Groups recognize and grieve their dead, or react if they turned.
- Prisoner work: "work the garden" waters your plants if they fear you.


v0.3.3 (weak-model understanding)
